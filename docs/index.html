What we get up to

🎨

Arts & Crafts

Creative play with paints, play-dough, glitter and more. We embrace the mess!

📚

Story Time

Shared stories, songs and rhymes to spark imagination and a love of books.

🎵

Music & Movement

Singing, dancing and instrument play to get little bodies moving and grooving.

🧩

Free Play

A wide range of age-appropriate toys, puzzles and games for independent exploration.

🌿

Outdoor Time

Weather permitting, we head outside for fresh air, running around and nature play.

☕

Parent Chat

Tea, coffee and biscuits while children play. Make new friends and share advice.
