We're so excited to announce our new **baby sensory corner**, running every Monday session from June onwards.

We know how tricky it can be with very young babies at a toddler group — so we've carved out a quiet, calm area just for them. Think:

- Soft, low lighting
- Mirror tiles for self-discovery
- Crinkle and texture fabrics
- Gentle musical toys
- Foam play mat for tummy time

The corner is open throughout the session and there's a comfortable chair nearby for feeding.

**A huge thank you** to our volunteers Jo and Priya who sourced and set up all the materials. You're stars. 🌟
