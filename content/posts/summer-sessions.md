Good news — we're keeping most of our sessions running throughout the school holidays this year. We know how important it is to have somewhere to go when the weeks stretch out!

## Holiday schedule

We'll be running every **Monday and Friday** throughout August, with a special outdoor session at Rowntree Park on **Wednesday 6th August** (weather permitting).

The Wednesday Acomb sessions will pause from 24 July to 20 August inclusive, resuming as normal on **Wednesday 27 August**.

## Summer special: water play

On the last Friday of July and August we'll be setting up our outdoor water play station. Bring a change of clothes and prepare to get a bit soggy!

See you there. 🌈
