Sunflower Playgroup has been welcoming families to York since 2018. We're a volunteer-run group offering a safe, warm and stimulating environment for children aged 0–5 and their parents, grandparents and carers.

## What we believe

We believe every child deserves the chance to play, explore and make friends in a nurturing setting. And every parent deserves a proper cup of tea and a friendly face.

Our sessions are deliberately relaxed. There's no curriculum, no pressure and no Pinterest-perfect expectations. Children lead the play, and grown-ups get a much-needed breather.

## Who runs us

Sunflower is run entirely by a small team of dedicated volunteers — many of whom started as parents attending sessions themselves. We're DBS-checked, first aid trained and passionate about early childhood.

Looking to volunteer, donate toys or sponsor a session? We'd love to hear from you. Drop us an email at [hello@terringtonplaygroup.co.uk](mailto:hello@terringtonplaygroup.co.uk).
